# Off-Page SEO Plan for Arixa Technologies

Off-page SEO is not only code. Use this plan after launch to build trust, citations, links, and brand signals.

## Backlink Strategy

- Publish useful service and blog content first so outreach has strong destination pages.
- Build links to service pages and high-value articles.
- Prioritize relevant business, SaaS, web design, startup, local business, and technology sites.
- Avoid paid link farms, spam directories, automated comments, and irrelevant guest posts.

## Local Citations

Create consistent profiles with the same company name, phone, website, description, and service categories:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Justdial or relevant regional directories
- Clutch
- GoodFirms
- DesignRush
- LinkedIn company page
- Facebook page
- Instagram business profile

## Social Profile Linking

- Link all social profiles back to the website.
- Link the website footer to official profiles.
- Keep name, logo, description, and services consistent.
- Post service updates, articles, launch notes, and client education content.

## Content Amplification

- Convert every blog into LinkedIn posts, carousels, short videos, and newsletter snippets.
- Share service explainers with local business groups and founder communities.
- Use internal links from new articles to relevant service pages and guides.

## Guest Post Strategy

- Target business, startup, marketing, design, and technology publications.
- Pitch practical topics such as website ROI, technical SEO, custom software workflows, and AI automation for small businesses.
- Link naturally to a service page or relevant guide.

## Google Business Profile

- Add services, business hours, website, logo, photos, and service areas.
- Post weekly updates.
- Request reviews after completed projects.
- Add FAQs and keep contact details consistent.

## Review Acquisition

- Ask satisfied clients for reviews on Google, Clutch, GoodFirms, LinkedIn, or relevant directories.
- Provide a short review prompt but never script fake reviews.
- Respond to reviews professionally.

## Partnership and PR Ideas

- Partner with local agencies, accountants, consultants, and business coaches.
- Offer co-branded webinars on websites, SEO, automation, and software planning.
- Publish launch announcements and expert articles.
- Contribute expert quotes to journalists and industry roundups.

## Measurement

Track:

- Referring domains
- Brand searches
- Google Business Profile actions
- Organic leads
- Service page rankings
- Blog impressions and clicks
- Conversion rate from organic pages
